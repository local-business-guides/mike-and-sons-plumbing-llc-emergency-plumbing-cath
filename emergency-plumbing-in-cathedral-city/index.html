What an urgent plumbing visit can involve

An emergency visit generally starts with describing the symptoms and where they’re happening. The plumber may need to inspect the affected fixture or pipe, identify the source, and explain the options. The exact work depends on the findings, so don’t assume every problem has the same repair or timeline.

Common terms you may hear include:
- Shutoff valve: a control that stops water flowing to a fixture or part of a plumbing system.
- Drain blockage: an obstruction that slows or stops wastewater from moving through a drain.
- Estimate: an explanation of expected charges for proposed work. Ask what it includes and what could change it.

Before calling, if it’s safe, note whether water is still running, which fixture is affected, and when the issue began. If you know where a shutoff valve is and can reach it safely, turning it off may limit water flow while you wait. Don’t take apart pipes or enter a wet area with electrical hazards.
